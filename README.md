## 📋 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to derive actionable insights for business decision-making. The analysis covers revenue patterns, customer segmentation, product performance, and the impact of discounts and subscriptions.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
1. Revenue generated by male vs female customers
2. High-value customers using discounts effectively
3. Top 5 products with highest average ratings
4. Shipping type preferences and spending patterns
5. Subscription impact on customer spending
6. Products with highest discount usage
7. Customer segmentation (New, Returning, Loyal)
8. Top 3 products per category
9. Relationship between repeat purchases and subscriptions
10. Revenue contribution by age group

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **SQL (PostgreSQL)**: Data extraction and complex queries
- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)**: Data cleaning and exploratory analysis
- **Power BI**: Interactive dashboard and visualization
